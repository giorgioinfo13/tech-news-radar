Privacy and personal data

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Tech News Radar is a private news reader, restricted to people approved by the administrator. The operator is Giorgio. For information, access to your data, corrections or deletion, contact giorgioinfo13@gmail.com.

Google sign-in and data use

Sign-in uses Google and Supabase. Only your email address and basic Google profile information are requested: your name, profile picture if present, and account identifiers. Supabase manages this data, identity verification and sign-in sessions. Tech News Radar does not receive your Google password and does not request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts or your calendar.

Identity data is used to recognize you, check your invitation and permissions, maintain your session and link followed sources and reading status to your profile. It is not used for advertising, sold or used to train artificial intelligence models. The app does not include behavioral analytics or advertising trackers.

Invitations and data stored in the app

Before you first sign in, the administrator may record your name and Google email address to invite you. The invitation also records who created it, its creation date and, after use, the acceptance date and the identifier of the account that accepted it. No email is sent automatically. Invitations are visible only to the administrator.

The app database stores your profile identifier and its link to your Supabase account, display name, role, access status and creation date. It also stores the sources you choose to follow, when you followed them, and articles marked as read with their reading dates. A new reader profile is created after the invitation is verified. A Google sign-in without approval may still create an authentication account in Supabase, without granting access to the news.

Source proposals

When you suggest a source, we store its name, optional URL, note, suggested category, and your choice to follow it after approval. We also store your profile reference and the submission date. Only you and the administrator can see the proposal. The administrator can see your display name.

We keep the status, reason, decision date, decision author, and approved source reference. Submission does not start collection. Results remain available in the app. We send no email or external notifications. Do not include personal or confidential data in notes.

Cancellation keeps the proposal and its result. Proposals have no automatic expiry. They remain until profile deletion or removal by the operator on request. An approved source in the shared catalog remains available.

Shared AI digest

The service integrates directly with the Google Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite API. Generation is disabled by default. The operator can enable it for controlled manual tests. Automatic generation requires separate activation. When generation is disabled, the app sends no requests to Gemini and saved digests remain available.

When generation is active, including during a manual test, the paid Google API receives only public RSS titles, descriptions, and article identifiers. Each attempt can include one token-count request and one generation request, with at most one attempt per hourly slot across the catalog. Public news can contain personal data about people in the news. User Google identities, email addresses, tokens, followed sources, and reading history are not sent. The app does not retrieve full articles. Opening, refreshing, or reading saved digests never generates an inference.

Approved users share Italian summaries and references. The app keeps these and attempt records for seven days. Cleanup runs on the next read or invocation. The monthly monetary ledger contains no user identity and remains stored to prevent budget reuse. Article retention does not change. Backups follow the separate rules below.

The paid service terms state that Google does not use prompts and responses to improve its products. The abuse monitoring policy provides for 55-day retention of prompts, context, and output. Authorized staff can review flagged content. Data can support policy enforcement systems, including their training. This is not ZDR, a promise of no training of any kind, or an EU-only storage guarantee. Summaries can contain errors. Check the original sources.

Who can access the data

Sources, categories, articles and collection history are shared among authorized users. Followed sources and reading states are personal and are not shown to other readers. The operator can access data through administration and database tools for support, maintenance, backups and handling data requests.

The service uses Vercel to host the website and forward requests, Render to process API requests, and Supabase for authentication and the database. These providers process the data needed for their respective functions and may record IP addresses, request times, browser information and other technical data for operation and security. Google manages authentication under its own terms.

You can read the providers’ privacy notices: Google, Supabase, Vercel and Render. Opening an article takes you to the source’s website, which receives your request and applies its own privacy notice. Server-side feed collection does not send your Google account or personal reading history to sources.

Browser and installed app

The browser stores session data and tokens to keep you signed in, as well as your theme and language preferences. Sign-out applies to the session in that browser or installed app: it does not automatically sign out other devices or delete data stored on the server. Theme and language preferences remain after sign-out and are not saved in your profile on the server. You can remove local data in your browser or installed app settings.

The browser and installed app may have separate sessions. The app requires a connection and does not provide an offline copy of the news. It does not set advertising cookies; Google and other providers may use cookies or similar technologies on their own pages.

Data retention

Profiles and invitations do not expire automatically. A pending invitation remains until it is used or canceled; a used invitation remains linked to the account until deletion. Profile and authentication data remains until the operator removes it, even if access has been revoked.

You can change followed sources and mark articles as unread. Unfollowing a source does not delete your reading states. These are deleted when you mark an article as unread, when the article is removed from the catalog, or when the profile is deleted.

Backups are stored separately and may contain personal data. No automatic expiry is currently configured for these copies. Providers’ technical log retention depends on the service and its configuration. Contact the operator to find out which retention periods apply to your data.

Requests and deletion

You can request information, a copy of your data, corrections or deletion by writing to the contact above, even if you cannot sign in. The operator verifies that the request comes from the person concerned. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

Deletion requires separate actions on the app profile and the Supabase authentication account. The operator revokes access and removes the profile, followed sources, reading states, associated invitations and authentication account, also checking for pending invitations. Shared news remains available to other readers. Deletion does not delete your Google account.

Removal from the active service does not automatically remove data from existing backups. The operator informs you of the deletion timeline and reapplies deletion requests before bringing a restored backup back into service. Revoking the Tech News Radar connection in your Google account settings does not replace a request to delete data already stored in the app.
